Hi, I'm Massoda — Visual Artist & Animation Creator

I paint, draw, and build worlds — mostly in Procreate and Procreate Dreams, sometimes in DaVinci Resolve when a piece needs to move further than a loop. My work ranges from character portraits and logo animation to Disney-inspired adverts, and it's all rooted in the same habit: sketch first, let the idea prove itself, then push it to a finished piece.

Fauxverse is my animation studio — home to original stories, characters, and a basketball universe unlike anything you've seen. It started as a place to keep the ideas I wasn't ready to let go of, and it's slowly becoming a real body of work: an animated series, a side-character story, and an illustrated book are all in motion right now.

Outside of the personal universe-building, I take on commission work — illustration, logo design and animation, and short-form video — for anyone who wants their brand or story told with the same care.

about
my art

I'm a self-taught visual artist and animation creator based in the digital space. My work lives at the intersection of character design, storytelling, and motion — built frame by frame in Procreate and Procreate Dreams.

From detailed illustrations and logo animations to full Disney-inspired animated adverts, every piece begins with a feeling and ends with a world. My animation studio, Fauxverse, is the umbrella for all my original stories and characters.
