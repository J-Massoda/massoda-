Why choose this service

The Difference

Most ML projects stall between experimentation and production. A model that scores well in a notebook is not the same as a model that serves reliably at scale, degrades gracefully under distribution shift, and can be retrained without breaking downstream systems.

I close that gap by treating deployability as a first-class requirement — every model is engineered with versioning, monitoring, and rollback in mind from the first training run. The result is an ML system your team can operate, trust, and iterate on without continuous intervention.
